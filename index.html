My Story

I am currently based in Cork, Ireland, where I am pursuing an MSc in Information Systems for Business Performance at University College Cork, while continuing to work remotely with a fintech company across the Middle East and Africa.

I was born and raised in Kuwait in the Middle east, and over the last five years I have worked closely with technology and payments, helping businesses build digital experiences that are reliable, simple to use, and ready to scale. Much of my work has involved payment gateways, cards, wallets, subscriptions, and fraud prevention, across websites, mobile apps, and enterprise systems.

Alongside my studies, I now work as a Technical Support Engineer at MoneyHash, where I help merchants design and implement payment flows that actually work in real world conditions. I enjoy breaking down complex systems and turning them into solutions that make sense for both businesses and users.

Over time, I see myself growing into a Solutions Architect role.
